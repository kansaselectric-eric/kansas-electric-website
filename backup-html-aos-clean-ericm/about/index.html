Our Story

Kansas Electric was founded in 1948 with one mission—deliver safe, reliable power with integrity. What started as a small electrical contractor has grown into a regional powerhouse.

Today, we build, automate, and maintain complex electrical systems across 11 states. From mission-critical environments to industrial-scale infrastructure, our customers rely on us to deliver power without compromise.
