Position Overview

Join Kansas Electric as a licensed Journeyman Electrician and take your career to the next level. Work on complex industrial and commercial electrical systems, lead projects, and mentor apprentices while enjoying competitive compensation and benefits.

Key Responsibilities

Professional electrical expertise and leadership

Installation & Maintenance

Perform electrical installations, maintenance, and troubleshooting on complex industrial and commercial systems

Blueprint Reading

Read and interpret electrical blueprints, schematics, and technical documentation with precision

Code Compliance

Ensure all work meets NEC and local electrical codes while maintaining the highest safety standards

Mentorship

Mentor and train apprentice electricians, sharing knowledge and developing the next generation

Requirements

  • Valid Kansas electrical license

  • Strong NEC knowledge

    Current National Electrical Code understanding

    Professional quality tools and equipment
