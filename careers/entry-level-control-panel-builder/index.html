Position Overview

We are seeking a motivated Entry-Level Control Panel Builder to join our growing automation team. You will learn to assemble, wire, and test UL 508A control panels that support industrial automation applications, working alongside experienced panel builders and automation engineers.

Key Responsibilities

Hands-on panel assembly and testing

Panel Assembly

Assist building UL 508A-certified control panels

Wiring & Termination

Route, label, crimp, and terminate per UL and company standards

Testing & QA

Support quality checks and functional testing of completed panels

Documentation

Maintain accurate work documentation; collaborate to meet schedule and quality goals

  • Read and interpret electrical schematics, wiring diagrams, and layout drawings
  • Mount components: breakers, relays, terminal blocks, VFDs, PLCs, power supplies
  • Participate in ongoing training in electrical assembly, controls, and automation technologies
  • Maintain automation department inventory processes and assist with automation projects
  • Keep work areas organized, safe, and compliant with safety standards

Qualifications

Required and preferred credentials

Required

  • High school diploma or equivalent (technical or vocational coursework preferred)
  • Basic understanding of electrical components and wiring
  • Ability to read simple diagrams or willingness to learn
  • Strong attention to detail and commitment to quality
  • Reliable, punctual, and able to follow safety procedures

Preferred

  • 0–2 years’ experience in a panel shop, electrical assembly, or industrial wiring
  • Familiarity with UL 508A and NFPA 70 (NEC)
  • Experience using hand tools, wire strippers, drills, and measuring instruments
  • Technical school certificate or associate degree in Industrial Automation, Electrical Technology, or Mechatronics

Compensation & Benefits

Competitive package with growth opportunities

Compensation

  • $18-$22/hr with overtime opportunities

Benefits

  • Full benefits package (health, dental, retirement, PTO)
  • On-the-job training with UL 508A panel builders and automation engineers
  • A collaborative, safety-first work environment focused on professional growth
