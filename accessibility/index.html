Accessibility Statement

Our Commitment

Kansas Electric is committed to providing a website that is accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of technology or ability. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA.

Measures We Take

  • Use of semantic HTML and appropriate ARIA attributes
  • Keyboard navigability and visible focus indicators
  • Text alternatives for non-text content where applicable
  • Color contrast checks and scalable typography
  • Regular reviews and incremental improvements

Conformance Status

The goal for this website is WCAG 2.2 Level AA conformance. We are working to improve areas that may not yet fully meet the standard and welcome your feedback.

Feedback & Contact

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on our site, please let us know and we will make reasonable efforts to address it promptly.

  • Phone: (316) 283-4750
  • Online: Contact form

Technical Specifications

Accessibility of this website relies on HTML, WAI-ARIA, CSS, and JavaScript. These technologies are relied upon for conformance with WCAG guidelines.

Assessment Approach

We assess accessibility through internal reviews, automated checks, and user feedback to guide continuous improvement.
