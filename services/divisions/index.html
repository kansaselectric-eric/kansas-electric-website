    Kansas Electric Service Divisions

    Our specialized service divisions deliver tailored electrical solutions for diverse industries and applications.

    Kansas Electric's service capabilities are organized into four specialized divisions, each with unique expertise and focused services. This divisional structure ensures that our clients receive tailored solutions and work with teams that thoroughly understand their specific industry challenges.

    Industrial Electrical Division

    Our Industrial Division specializes in complex electrical systems for manufacturing plants, food processing facilities, oil & gas infrastructure, and other industrial settings.

    • Advanced Manufacturing Facilities
    • Wastewater & Water Treatment
    • Food & Beverage Processing
    • Oil, Gas & Carbon Capture
    • Microgrids & Energy Management
    Explore Industrial Division

    Commercial Electrical Division

    Our Commercial Division delivers electrical solutions for office buildings, retail centers, municipal facilities, and public infrastructure projects.

    • Large-Scale Commercial Construction
    • Water Towers & Municipal Infrastructure
    • Airport Electrical Infrastructure
    • Educational & Healthcare Facilities
    • LED & Energy-Efficient Lighting
    Explore Commercial Division

    Service & Maintenance Division

    Our Service Division provides 24/7 emergency support and preventative maintenance services to ensure electrical system reliability and safety.

    • 24/7 Emergency Electrical Service
    • Infrared (IR) Scans & Predictive Maintenance
    • Arc Flash Studies & NFPA 70E Compliance
    • NFPA 70B Maintenance Program Implementation
    • Breaker Testing & Power System Testing
    Explore Service Division

    Automation & Control Division

    Our Automation Division creates smart industrial control systems that improve efficiency, quality, and productivity for manufacturing and process facilities.

    • UL508A Panel Building & Fabrication
    • PLC Programming & Industrial Automation
    • Control Panel Troubleshooting & Upgrades
    • Remote Monitoring & Smart Factory Solutions
    • SCADA & HMI Development
    Explore Automation Division

    Cross-Divisional Collaboration

    While each division has its specific focus and expertise, our teams frequently collaborate on complex projects that require diverse skill sets. This integrated approach allows us to deliver comprehensive solutions that address all aspects of your electrical needs, from initial design to ongoing maintenance.

    For projects that span multiple divisions, we assign a single project manager to coordinate all work, ensuring seamless communication and consistent quality throughout the project lifecycle.
