WHAT WE DELIVER

Turnkey NFPA 70B Electrical Maintenance Program

From discovery to documentation, we stand up a complete EMP program and keep it current.

Baseline Assessment

Asset inventory, one‑line validation, nameplate capture, and condition review to establish scope and risk.

Criticality Analysis

Consequence/likelihood scoring to prioritize assets and set maintenance intervals per NFPA 70B guidance.

EPM Procedures

Task lists and methods for switchgear, breakers, transformers, MCCs, VFDs, generators, ATS, and distribution.

Condition‑Based Testing

IR thermography, ultrasonic, insulation resistance, DLRO, power quality, partial discharge, oil analysis, and arc‑flash study coordination/updates.

Breaker & Switchgear Service

Cleaning, lubrication, torqueing, contact resistance, trip testing, settings capture, and corrective repairs.

Documentation & CMMS

Asset registry, procedure library, schedules, findings, photos, and evidence logs compatible with your CMMS.

COMPLIANCE + SAFETY

We ensure your maintenance program reinforces the protections assumed in your arc‑flash labels and coordination study. Where data is missing, we help you close gaps and set a maintainable baseline.
