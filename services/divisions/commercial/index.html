Commercial Electrical Division

Building the infrastructure that powers communities and businesses throughout Kansas and beyond.

Kansas Electric's Commercial Electrical Division specializes in the design, installation, and maintenance of electrical systems for a wide range of commercial and public infrastructure projects. Our team brings decades of experience to every project, ensuring safe, code-compliant, and efficient electrical solutions that meet the unique needs of each commercial client.

Commercial Project Expertise

Our Commercial Electrical Division has successfully completed projects of all sizes, from small business renovations to major infrastructure developments. We understand the unique challenges of commercial construction, including tight schedules, budget constraints, and the coordination required with multiple trades and stakeholders.

Our Approach to Commercial Projects

  • Collaborative Planning: Working closely with architects, general contractors, and project managers from the earliest stages
  • Code Compliance: Expert knowledge of national, state, and local electrical codes and regulations
  • Value Engineering: Identifying opportunities to optimize electrical designs for cost-effectiveness without compromising quality
  • Schedule Adherence: Dedicated project managers who keep commercial electrical work on-track and coordinate with other trades
  • Quality Installation: Skilled electricians who take pride in their workmanship and attention to detail
  • Responsive Service: Ongoing support for modifications, expansions, and maintenance after project completion
