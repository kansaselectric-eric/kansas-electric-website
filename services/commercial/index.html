    Electrical Services Commercial

    Our committed team of commercial electricians is ready to provide solutions for your facility's electrical needs. We value the opportunity to meet with you and evaluate how our electricians will enhance the efficiency and safety of your commercial operations. You can rely on us to deliver tailored solutions that meet your specific electrical needs, ensuring optimal performance for your facility.

    • We handle all aspects of electrical systems in new construction projects. Our design-build approach ensures seamless coordination from concept to completion. From planning and design to installation and testing, we guarantee efficient, safe, and code-compliant electrical solutions. We'll also provide comprehensive training and ongoing support to ensure your systems operate smoothly for years to come.

    • We inspect, repair, and upgrade your electrical systems to ensure safety and reliability in buildings or facilities.

    • We know the transmission of electrical power is vital to keep your facility running. We offer ongoing maintenance and upgrades to ensure a reliable power distribution infrastructure.

      • Motor Controls Motor controls utilize devices such as starters, contactors, and variable frequency drives. Integrating these electrical components effectively controls motor operation to meet your needs. The result is improved energy efficiency and enhanced system reliability across diverse applications.
      • Thermal Imaging Study

        We will help you predetermine potential electrical faults by using state-of-the-art infrared cameras to detect and visualize thermal patterns on your electrical systems. The resulting infrared images, or thermograms, show variations in temperature that may indicate issues such as:

        • Loose connections
        • Insulation deficiencies
        • Equipment degradation

        We will provide you with an organized report that you can use as a baseline reference. Many of our customers do this study on an annual basis.

      • Transformer Oil Sample Testing

        We will collect and analyze oil samples from your transformers to determine their health and efficiency. This preventative maintenance service has been most effective in predicting potential transformer catastrophic failures. Early detection will extend the lifespan of your transformers and prevent costly downtime.

      • Breaker Exercising

        Another preventative maintenance service we offer is breaker exercising. This service includes opening and closing circuit breakers under controlled conditions to verify their performance and reliability. Breaker exercising helps identify issues such as:

        • Corrosion
        • Contamination
        • Mechanical deterioration that can affect the breaker's ability to offer reliable overcurrent protection.
      • Switchgear Cleaning

        We offer routine Switchgear cleaning. This involves removing dirt and contaminants from electrical switchgear. We are experienced at cleaning your materials using proper PPE to prevent arc faults. Regular cleaning is essential for the reliability and safety of electrical systems.

      • Engineered Arc Flash Study

        We provide arc flash studies to comply with safety and insurance standards. This is a systematic analysis of your electrical system to identify the risks associated with arc flash incidents. Key steps:

        • Data collection
        • Arc flash hazard analysis in conjunction with an electrical engineer
        • Applying arc flash labeling to your electrical equipment, detailing protective measures (proper PPE needed)

    • We evaluate the condition and capacity of your existing electrical service. This involves:

      • Replacing panels
      • Upgrading wiring
      • Ensuring adherence to codes
      • Performing safety and functionality tests.

    • We ensure the reliability of surveillance camera systems to give you peace of mind.
