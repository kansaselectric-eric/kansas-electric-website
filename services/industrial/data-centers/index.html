Data Centers & Mission Critical Facilities

Specialized electrical systems for data centers and mission-critical infrastructure, ensuring maximum uptime and reliability.

Reliability & Redundancy

Our N+1 and 2N redundant power systems ensure continuous operation even during utility outages. We design and implement backup power solutions, UPS systems, and emergency power off (EPO) systems that keep your critical infrastructure running 24/7/365.

Energy Efficiency

We implement energy-efficient designs that reduce operational costs while maintaining reliability. Our solutions include high-efficiency UPS systems, intelligent PDUs, and advanced cooling infrastructure integration that optimize power usage effectiveness (PUE).

Scalability & Future-Proofing

Our modular design approach allows your infrastructure to grow seamlessly with your business. We implement solutions that can be expanded without disrupting operations, ensuring your facility can adapt to increasing power demands.

Monitoring & Management

We integrate advanced power monitoring systems that provide real-time data on electrical performance, helping you optimize operations and identify potential issues before they affect uptime. Our solutions include DCIM integration for comprehensive facility management.

Comprehensive Data Center Electrical Services

Design & Engineering

Our experienced engineers design electrical systems that meet the unique requirements of your data center or mission-critical facility. We consider factors such as redundancy, efficiency, and scalability to create solutions that align with industry standards and best practices.

Power Distribution

We install robust power distribution systems, including switchgear, PDUs, and branch circuits, ensuring reliable power delivery throughout your facility. Our solutions include overhead busway systems and underfloor distribution options tailored to your specific needs.

Backup Power Systems

Our backup power solutions include UPS systems, generator installations, and automatic transfer switches that ensure continuous operation during utility outages. We implement redundant configurations that eliminate single points of failure in your critical power infrastructure.

Preventive Maintenance

Regular maintenance is essential for maintaining reliability in mission-critical environments. Our comprehensive maintenance programs include scheduled inspections, testing, and servicing of all electrical components to prevent unexpected failures and extend equipment life.

System Upgrades & Retrofits

As your facility evolves, we help you upgrade and retrofit existing electrical systems to accommodate increased power demands or improve efficiency. Our experienced team implements changes with minimal disruption to your operations.
