Food & Beverage Electrical Expertise

Powering production with cleanliness, reliability, and controls that meet industry demands.

Clean, Code-Compliant Electrical for Food Production

Kansas Electric specializes in delivering washdown-ready electrical installations for food and beverage manufacturing facilities. Our team understands the unique requirements of food-safe environments, from stainless steel conduit systems to segregated power distribution that maintains sanitary standards.

We design and install integrated automation systems for packaging, bottling, batching, and sorting operations, ensuring your production lines run efficiently while meeting stringent food safety regulations. Our UL-certified control panels and facility-wide SCADA systems provide the reliability and monitoring capabilities essential for modern food production.

From initial design through commissioning and ongoing maintenance, Kansas Electric delivers electrical solutions that keep your food production moving safely and efficiently.
