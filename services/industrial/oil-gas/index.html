Oil, Gas & Carbon Capture

Powering critical energy infrastructure with expert midstream electrical services.

Turnkey Electrical Partner for Midstream Operations

Kansas Electric is a trusted electrical contracting partner for North America's energy sector, delivering comprehensive midstream electrical services that power the nation's infrastructure. We specialize in providing end-to-end solutions for pipeline automation controls, ensuring seamless and efficient operations across vast networks. Our expertise extends to complete compressor station power systems, from initial design and hazardous location compliance to final commissioning. We are proficient in advanced SCADA system integration, enabling robust remote monitoring and control that enhances safety and operational uptime.

Our project portfolio includes electrical contracting for tank farms, greenfield carbon capture facility design, and brownfield retrofits. We understand the critical importance of reliability in this sector, which is why our services include redundant power systems, emergency backup solutions, and 24/7 maintenance support. With a deep understanding of industry regulations and a commitment to safety, Kansas Electric is equipped to handle the unique electrical challenges of the oil, gas, and carbon capture industries, ensuring your projects are completed on time, within budget, and to the highest standards of quality.
