Turnkey Electrical Partner for Midstream Operations

Kansas Electric is a trusted electrical contracting partner for North America's energy sector, delivering comprehensive midstream electrical services that power the nation's infrastructure. We specialize in providing end-to-end solutions for pipeline automation controls, ensuring seamless and efficient operations across vast networks.

Our expertise extends to complete compressor station power systems, advanced SCADA integration, redundant backup systems, tank farms, and carbon capture design. With a deep understanding of industry regulations and a commitment to safety, Kansas Electric ensures your midstream projects are completed on time, on budget, and built to the highest standards.

All electrical and instrumentation work for Southern Star's Princeton receipt interconnect, supporting critical natural gas transmission infrastructure.
